01 · Motivation

At 10 m, the details that matter disappear.

Sentinel-2 images every land surface on Earth every five days, free of charge. That makes it the default source for agricultural, urban and disaster monitoring in India. But a 10 m pixel is wider than most rooftops, rural roads and field bunds.

Urban

Individual structures

Dense housing merges into uniform texture, limiting building counts and change detection.

Transport

Narrow roads

Roads narrower than a pixel fragment or vanish from extracted networks.

Agriculture

Field boundaries

Most Indian holdings are under one hectare. The bunds between them are only metres wide.

Disaster

Localised damage

Landslide scarps, breached embankments and flood margins blur into their surroundings.

02 · Method

Five stages from archive to analysis-ready product.

The same pipeline runs as a Python batch job and entirely inside the browser. On identical scenes the two implementations agree to within 0.05 dB.

  1. 1

    Acquire

    Queries the Sentinel-2 L2A archive for the location, then ranks candidate scenes by the cloud-free fraction of the target window.

    STACSCL mask
  2. 2

    Prepare

    Reads only the required window from cloud-optimised GeoTIFFs, harmonises processing baselines and stacks ten bands.

    COGL2A reflectance
  3. 3

    Super-resolve

    A compact network predicts 2.5 m detail. A frequency-domain constraint then restores the input's low-frequency content exactly.

    ONNX×4
  4. 4

    Quantify

    Eight rotated and mirrored inferences give a per-pixel uncertainty. The output is also re-checked against the input.

    Ensemble σSelf-consistency
  5. 5

    Deliver

    GeoTIFFs in the source UTM grid, an uncertainty layer, validation metrics and an interactive comparison.

    GeoTIFFEPSG

Radiometry preserved by construction

Degrade a 2.5 m product and you recover the measured 10 m input at over 49 dB. NDVI and other indices remain valid.

Uncertainty reported, not hidden

Reconstructed detail is flagged wherever the model disagrees with itself, so it is never mistaken for observation.

Runs where analysts work

A 19 MB model with no server dependency. It works on a standard laptop, offline once cached, and suits air-gapped networks.

05 · Validation

Measured against what the satellite recorded.

No 2.5 m reference exists for an arbitrary scene, so every product is tested for self-consistency. It is degraded back to each band's native resolution and compared with the original measurement.

Self-consistency shows that no measured information was altered. It does not prove that the reconstructed detail is correct. Benchmarking against independent high-resolution references (OpenSR-test, SEN2NAIP cross-sensor pairs) is part of the v2 plan.
