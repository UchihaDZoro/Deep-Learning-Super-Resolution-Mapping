01 · The problem

Ten metres hides what decisions need.

Sentinel-2 revisits every five days, globally and for free. But at 10 m one pixel covers a small house, and the details analysts act on blur away.

URBAN

Small buildings

Individual rooftops merge into a single texture.

TRANSPORT

Narrow roads

Lanes under 10 m wide break up or vanish.

AGRICULTURE

Field boundaries

Smallholder bunds in India are only a few metres wide.

DISASTER

Local damage

Landslide edges, breached banks and flood lines.

M. Chinnaswamy Stadium, Bengaluru · Sentinel-2 L2A, 13 Mar 2024. Same scene, same colour stretch.

02 · Method

From orbit to 2.5 m in five steps.

One pipeline, written twice: in Python for batch production and in the browser for the live lab. Both produce identical numbers.

1

Acquire

STAC search for Sentinel-2 L2A over the point. Picks the clearest window using the scene-classification cloud mask.

Earth SearchSCL
2

Prepare

Reads only the needed window from cloud-optimised GeoTIFFs. Harmonises the baseline-04 offset and stacks 10 bands.

COGBOA
3

Super-resolve

Lightweight SR network, tiled with feathered overlaps. A low-frequency hard constraint pins radiometry to the input.

ONNXWASM
4

Trust layer

A dihedral ensemble gives a per-pixel σ. The output is degraded back to 10 m and 20 m and checked against the input.

TTAPSNR·SAM
5

Deliver

2.5 m GeoTIFFs in the original UTM grid, plus an uncertainty band, metrics and an interactive comparison.

GeoTIFFEPSG

Physics-consistent

Degrade the 2.5 m output and you recover the observed 10 m input (>49 dB). NDVI and other indices stay scientifically valid.

Honest about inference

Every product ships with an uncertainty map, so reconstructed detail is never mistaken for observed detail.

Deployable anywhere

A 19 MB model with no server. It runs air-gapped on an analyst's laptop, and the same code runs as a batch job.

      TRINETRA-SR

      Deep-learning super-resolution mapping from medium-resolution satellite imagery. Smart India Hackathon, problem statement 26142, National Technical Research Organisation.

      Data & models

      • Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data
      • Live scenes: Sentinel-2 COGs on AWS via Element 84 Earth Search
      • Presets: Microsoft Planetary Computer
      • Baseline network: SEN2SR-Lite (ESA OpenSR)
      • Earth textures: NASA Blue Marble via three.js
