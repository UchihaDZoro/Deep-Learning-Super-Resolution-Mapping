Pipeline

01

Acquire

STAC search for Sentinel-2 L2A over the AOI. Clearest scene picked with the SCL cloud mask. Baseline-04 harmonisation.

02

Pre-process

10 bands (10 m + 20 m) stacked on the 10 m grid and scaled to reflectance. Tiled with overlap.

03

Super-resolve

Lightweight network with a low-frequency hard constraint → 2.5 m output with radiometry preserved.

04

Trust layer

8-fold dihedral ensemble → per-pixel σ. Consistency, spectral-angle and NDVI checks.

05

Deliver

2.5 m GeoTIFF (same CRS) + uncertainty band + metrics → web viewer / GIS.

What makes it different

Per-pixel trust map LIVE

Every 2.5 m product ships with an uncertainty band that flags where detail is inferred rather than observed. v2: conformal calibration with guaranteed coverage.

Physics-consistent radiometry LIVE

Degrade the output back to 10 m and it matches the input. NDVI and other indices stay scientifically valid.

All-band 2.5 m LIVE

Red-edge and SWIR are sharpened too, not just RGB. That gives NDVI, NDRE, NDWI and NBR at 2.5 m for crops, water and burn scars.

Multi-temporal fusion v2

Fuse several 5-day revisits so extra detail is partly observed (sub-pixel shifts), not only imagined.

Fidelity ↔ Detail dial v2

Deterministic fidelity mode for analysts, plus a diffusion refiner for detail. Blend them live and watch the uncertainty update.

Task-level validation v2

Buildings, roads, field boundaries and flood/landslide change detection on an India validation set, plus a phantom-object hallucination audit.
