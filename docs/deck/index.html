PROBLEM & SOLUTION OVERVIEW
PS 26142 · NTRO · SPACE TECHNOLOGY
Deep-learning super-resolution mapping from medium-resolution satellite imagery
The ask: Sentinel-2 10 m → below 4 m, consistent geometry & spectra, explicit uncertainty, HR validation.
PROBLEM
A 10 m pixel is wider than most roofs, lanes and field bunds, so fine-scale mapping, change detection and damage assessment suffer.
→
GAP
Commercial VHR imagery is costly and must be tasked. Generic SR hallucinates, alters radiometry and reports no confidence.
→
SOLUTION
TRINETRA-SR: physics-constrained 4× SR on all 10 bands, with a per-pixel trust map, running anywhere, even offline.
Proposed Solution (Describe your Idea/Solution/Prototype)
Detailed explanation of the proposed solution
  • Input: Sentinel-2 L2A, 10 bands, via cloud-aware search or a GeoTIFF upload.
  • Model: deep SR backbone + a low-frequency hard constraint (FFT), so it can add detail but never change measured radiometry.
  • Output: 2.5 m 10-band GeoTIFF, a σ-uncertainty GeoTIFF and validation metrics, in the source UTM grid.
How it addresses the problem
  • Roofs, stadiums, bunds and debris tracks become resolvable (1.17× edge energy vs bicubic).
  • Degrading the output reproduces the input at 51.1 dB (10 m bands) and 36.8 dB (20 m bands).
  • Every inferred detail is flagged by the per-pixel uncertainty map.

RESEARCH AND REFERENCES
Details / Links of the reference and research work
KEY FINDINGS → OUR DESIGN DECISIONS
SEN2SR [1]: a low-frequency hard constraint gives radiometric consistency; > 15 M parameters brought no significant gain.
→
Keep the constraint; compact backbone.
GeoSR-Bench [13]: PSNR/SSIM gains often do not track downstream-task gains.
→
Validate on buildings, roads, bunds.
LDSR-S2 [6]: the only prior S2 SR with pixel-wise uncertainty, but diffusion is slow.
→
TTA σ now; diffusion as optional refiner.
SEN2NAIP [9]: 2,851 real + 17,657 synthetic pairs; real pairs are mis-registered.
→
Synthetic pre-train, shift-tolerant tuning.
HighRes-net · WorldStrat · MuS2 [8, 10, 11]: revisits add true sub-pixel information.
→
Temporal attention front-end (v2).
